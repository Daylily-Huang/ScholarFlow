01 · 主线起点来自反复的词，而不是理论标签

先看谁把她当“没人”

论文列出 nobody、nothing、“只是安妮”等措辞。它们不是说她不存在，而是显示在父亲和姐妹眼里，她的愿望没有多少权重。弹琴时她支撑别人的快乐，自己却不被欣赏，进一步把这种处境演成了一个场面。

所以，安妮的成长不能只写成从幼稚到成熟：她27岁，早已有判断。问题是她是否拥有表达与被认真对待的空间。作者后面的住所、旁听和身体分析，都是为这条变化寻找证据。

先有被忽视的位置，才会理解她为什么“有房子住却没有家”。

02 · 住所的意义跟着关系变化

同一所房子，怎么先像监牢，后来又像家

父亲重视镜子和贵族名录，安妮留下的却是花园碎片。作者由物件与记忆读两种价值取向，而不是把房屋介绍当背景资料略过。频繁换住处，也使她缺少稳定归属。

更强的对照发生在卡姆登街：起初她预感数月“囚禁”，与温特沃思重聚后，同一地点却成了“回家”。地址不变，关系改变，家的意义便变了。结尾仍有海军生活与战争的不确定，不能把结婚简化成一切安稳。

03 · 抓住一个词，就能走进叙述视角

别人只看见行礼，我们却听见她松了多大一口气

重逢场景外表很短：半碰上目光、行礼、听见声音。安妮却觉得房间满是人声，紧张得只想熬过去。作者特别点出 seemed：房间“似乎”如此，提示这并非中性的布景盘点，而是安妮此刻的感受。

自由间接话语常保留第三人称叙述，却让人物的评价、口气进入句子；直接内心话则会明确引出人物心中原话。本文把两者放在相邻讨论里，读时仍应逐句区分。下面用它引用的一句短语演示：重复与 seem 怎样让几秒钟的会面充满主观压力。

04 · 旁听渠道怎样影响人物判断

听到的是原话，补出来的却可能是误会

安妮听见温特沃思谈路易莎的坚定，就以为他准备娶她。温特沃思又听见安妮可能嫁给表兄的传闻，因而嫉妒。句子被听见了，真实意图却没有机会被问清；两人的担忧便填进信息空白。

到白鹿旅店，位置倒转。安妮与哈维尔谈女性的爱情和发言机会，温特沃思在旁听见，写信向她表白。间接交流这次成功，说明问题不只在“偷听不好”，还在传来的究竟是流言、猜测，还是她清楚表达的信念，以及对方能否回应。

话语有时掩饰、有时不够，人物于是还得从身体反应读心；这条通道同样有力量，也有限度。

05 · 用身心联系读人物，也要读到矛盾

脸红、假笑、机械弹琴：心事怎样从身体露出来

安妮能看懂温特沃思一闪而过的嘴角、假笑和鄙视，熟悉使细微信号有了意义。读者也从她的脸红、呼吸和慌乱认识她；她一面应付谈话或弹琴，一面陷在心事中，呈现内外不一致的经验。

作者用浪漫主义时期的心理学讨论这类身心联系，研究对象仍是文学表现。重要的是，识别某个表情准确，不代表知道对方全部感情。她甚至在收到表白后，幸福得听不懂身边人说话：成长与暂时失去镇定并不冲突。

因此结尾的变化需要落在判断、行动和发言的关系上，而非一条不断上升的“读心能力值”。

06 · 把几条细读线收回来

从旁听到公开争辩，才看清 somebody 的分量

莱姆事故中，别人等她给指示，她的判断第一次以照顾和组织行动被明确认可。白鹿旅店里，她再追问：男人掌握教育和笔，现存故事怎能自动证明女人的天性？她不再只是被别人描述的人，也开始质疑描述的权力。

这就是作者把住所、旁听、视角、身体串起来的理由：社会上被忽视的人，先在叙述中拥有丰富内心，继而在场景里获得行动与发言分量。仍可保留另一种读法：认知未必一路扩展，结局也未消除社会和职业风险。要讨论这些分歧，就回到具体语句与场面比较，而不只互换理论名词。

原文与译文
The aim of this article is to substantiate the thesis that together with the development of the plot of Persuasion, the cognitive power of the principal heroine expands, and she becomes a highly sensitive reader of human minds. This thesis is supported by references to the new ‘Romantic’ psychology, emphasizing the close links between the innate aspects of the mind and the body. Psychological insight demonstrates the fragmentation of Anne Elliot’s mind, the role of the unconscious, and the division between the interior and the exterior. There is also analysis of the significance of Anne’s frequent change of transitory lodgings, along with interpretation of the narrative strategy, especially free indirect speech and mediated speech (the function of eavesdropping) and the important role of body language.
本文要支持这样一个判断：《劝导》的情节向前发展时，女主人公安妮·艾略特的认知能力也在扩展，她逐渐成为敏锐的他人心思观察者。文章借新的“浪漫主义”心理学强调心智的先天方面与身体之间的密切联系，讨论安妮心灵的分裂、无意识以及内外之分；同时分析她频繁更换临时住所的意义、自由间接话语和间接交流（尤其旁听）的叙事作用，以及身体语言的重要性。

白话解释

安妮话不多，故事也没有多少惊天动地的大事，可她一直在看、在猜、在难过。作者要你留心的就是这条暗线：她怎样理解别人，读者又怎样从她的反应里理解她。房子、偷听和脸红都成了证据。
逻辑要点
  • 情节推进伴随安妮认知能力的变化，构成全文待论证的中心判断。
  • 住所、话语和身体反应提供不同入口，共同解释这种变化如何被写出来。
  • 浪漫主义心理学为这些文本细节提供解释框架。

原文与译文
Persuasion is Jane Austen’s last completed novel and at the same time the most psychologically developed. The aim of this article is to present arguments for the thesis that in addition to the maturation of Anne Elliot, the novel represents an expansion of her cognitive powers, and she becomes a very sensitive reader of human minds. At the same time, readers learn more and more about Anne’s complicated psychological state. The new ‘Romantic’ psychology which emphasizes the links between the body and the mind, the physiological and the mental state, would have helped the reader to understand the complex inner state of the heroine (Cf. Juliet McMaster 2004; Hillman and Maude 2015; Richardson 2001, 2002; Smith 2017; Wiltshire 2003; Mullan 2005). In other words, this article is about how Anne Elliot ‘reads’ other characters and how she herself may be ‘read’.
《劝导》是简·奥斯汀最后完成的小说，也是其心理刻画最充分的一部。本文认为，小说不仅表现安妮·艾略特的成熟，也表现她认知能力的扩展：她成为非常敏锐的人心观察者；与此同时，读者越来越了解她复杂的心理状态。新的“浪漫主义”心理学强调身体与心灵、生理与心理状态的联系，有助于理解她的内心（参见 McMaster 2004；Hillman 与 Maude 2015；Richardson 2001、2002；Smith 2017；Wiltshire 2003；Mullan 2005）。换句话说，本文讨论安妮如何“读”别人，也讨论她自己如何被“读”。

白话解释

先别把“读心”想成超能力。比如对方嘴上客气，眼睛却露出不屑，安妮会从这点不一致判断他的态度。而我们看见安妮突然脸红，也会猜到她嘴上没说出的心事。文章同时追踪这两种阅读。
逻辑要点
  • 安妮观察他人，与读者理解安妮，形成相互配合的两个方向。
  • 把身体反应也当线索，才能看见仅靠人物对白看不到的心理活动。

原文与译文
In my reflections on body language in Persuasion I have been mainly inspired by the works of Alan Richardson, which were later discussed in Karah L. Smith’s article, ‘Cognitive Embodiment and Mind Reading in Jane Austen’s Persuasion’. Alan Richardson’s works are not only focused on body language but also on the state of brain science at the time and other aspects of Anne’s inner life, such as the fragmentation of her character, the role of the unconscious, and the division between the exterior and the interior. Karah L. Smith concentrates on the importance of body language in Persuasion and its presentation in the text. I agree with both scholars when they notice a close relation between the physiology of the body and the state of the characters’ minds and emotions. Both scholars point to the unique capacity of Anne Elliot as a mind reader. Moreover, Karah Smith rightly observes that reading minds is an imperfect tool, as Anne cannot be sure till the very end of the novel of Captain Wentworth’s love for her.
本文关于《劝导》中身体语言的思考，主要受 Alan Richardson 的研究启发，Karah L. Smith 后来在《简·奥斯汀〈劝导〉中的认知具身与读心》中讨论了这些研究。Richardson 不只研究身体语言，也关注当时的脑科学、安妮性格的分裂、无意识和内外之分；Smith 则集中于身体语言及其文本呈现。我赞同两位学者关于身体生理与人物心理、情绪紧密相连的判断。他们都指出安妮具有独特的识人能力；但正如 Smith 所说，这种能力并不完美，因为安妮直到小说接近结尾，仍无法确定温特沃思上校是否爱她。

这段先交代作者接着谁往下做，也把一个矛盾摆出来：安妮那么会看脸色，怎么还会猜不出自己最在乎的人？这个缺口很重要，后面的旁听与书信就是围着它展开的。

原文与译文
In my article, I am seeking to provide further evidence of Anne Elliot’s body language and inner states. I do not deal only with Anne’s mind reading and her observations of her own inward reactions but try to ‘read’ Anne’s character through other literary signals in the text, such as her frequent change of houses, that present her as a lonely, unstable character, lacking family support. In addition, the use of free indirect speech reveals Anne’s sensitivity, emotions, and her changeable moods. The frequent scenes of overhearing point to the restrictive role of a society with its decorum and the suppression of emotions, although sincerity and emotions become gradually more and more important.
本文进一步寻找安妮身体语言与内在状态的证据，不只研究她如何识人和观察自己的反应，也通过其他文学信号“读”她：频繁迁居显出她的孤独、不安定和家庭支持的缺失；自由间接话语呈现她的敏感、情绪和心境变化；反复出现的旁听场景则显示，社会礼仪与情绪压抑限制了交流，不过真诚和情感逐渐变得更重要。

白话解释

作者准备把几条线合着读：安妮住在哪里，说明她在别人生活里有多大位置；叙述怎样贴近她，说明我们能知道她多少心事；她只能旁听谁，又说明有些话为什么始终说不出口。
逻辑要点
  • 由身体语言扩大到住所与叙述形式，可以把私人感受放回社会关系。
  • 礼仪对交流的限制，解释了为什么观察、旁听和内心话如此重要。
  • 这些线索共同服务于安妮逐渐获得表达空间的主线。

原文与译文
Anne Elliot, the middle daughter of Sir Walter Elliot of Kellynch Hall, seems to be nobody in the eyes of her family. The words ‘nobody’ or ‘nothing’ indeed appear frequently, especially in the opening chapters of the novel. She ‘was nobody with either father or sister… she was only Anne’.1 Anne does not at first travel with her family to Bath ‘for nobody will want her in Bath’ (p. 31), Anne tries ‘not to be in the way of anybody’ (p. 82). To Elizabeth, Anne ‘is nothing… compared with you [Mrs. Clay]’ (p. 142). Anne is a Cinderella with two ‘ugly’ sisters. Her elder sister, Elizabeth, performs the role of the mistress of the house in Kellynch Hall; her younger sister, Mary, is already well married, so her social position is higher than Anne’s, according to contemporary standards. When their mother was alive, Kellynch Hall was Anne’s beloved home, but it is no surprise that after the death of her mother Anne could not feel happy there, nor treat it as her home. This aspect of the novel, Anne’s ‘homelessness’, in the sense that she does not have a real home, is visible and meaningful in the novel. As body language reveals quite a lot about characters and their emotions, so readers ‘read’ Anne’s character through the distinctive signals in the text. ‘Homelessness’ is one such meaningful signal for our understanding of the social situation at the time, but it also helps us to ‘read’ Anne’s character.
安妮是凯林奇府沃尔特爵士的二女儿，在家人眼里似乎无足轻重。小说开头常出现 nobody、nothing：父亲和姐姐都不把她当回事，“她只是安妮”（注1）；她不随家人先去巴斯，因为“那里没人需要她”（第31页）；她努力“不妨碍任何人”（第82页）；伊丽莎白说，与克莱夫人相比，安妮“什么也不是”（第142页）。她像有两个“丑姐姐”的灰姑娘：大姐在凯林奇主持家务，妹妹玛丽已嫁人，按当时标准地位都比她高。母亲在世时，她爱这个家；母亲死后，她已难感幸福，也难把这里当自己的家。这种没有真正归属的“无家可归”，既说明她的社会处境，也是读懂她的信号，正如身体语言能显露情绪一样。

白话解释

安妮不是没屋子住，而是每到一处都像“顺便收留的那个人”：用得着时叫她帮忙，用不着时没人想起她。作者盯住反复出现的“没人”“什么也不是”，因为这些小词把她在家里的分量写得很具体。

The episodes of overhearing occur at critical moments of the narrative. The first episode takes place when Anne meets her former fiancé at the Musgroves’ home. He pretends not to pay much attention to her, and she is very agitated when she overhears his conversation with other people: ‘When he talked, she heard the same voice, and discerned the same mind’ (p. 62). 原文与译文
Let us remember that the development of the heroine goes together with her development as the focalizer. Not given a voice in the initial chapters, Anne starts to talk in the third chapter of the novel, and she often becomes a focalizer, so that we observe the events of the plot also from her subjective point of view, through her free indirect speech. Sometimes she informs her readers about her nervousness or sadness or happiness, but we also get to know about her feelings through the language she uses. 原文与译文
Persuasion is an interesting novel as it introduces some innovations in Austen’s art. We cannot observe, as in her other novels, the maturing of the heroine, as Anne whom we meet at 27 years old is already a mature person, but we may observe that she changes and gains more experience. We can notice that she does not have support and respect from her family, and she is treated as ‘nobody’. She also seems to change her transitory houses quite frequently, and she has to wait for her own ‘home’ throughout the whole novel. After her broken engagement when she was 19, she has been waiting for the happy fulfilment of her love for a long time, being in a state of ‘in between’, very much in love with her former fiancé but uncertain about his feelings and so suffering anxiety and pain. Most of the ‘plot’ takes place in her mind, and among Austen’s heroines she is the one with the richest interior. Because of Austen’s expansive use of free indirect speech, she is the most psychologically developed of all the heroines of Austen’s novels. This is also a novel about difficulties with direct communication about emotions and a lot is revealed through overhearing and the final letter written by Wentworth, so through mediated forms of communication. This is also the novel in which the new empirical psychology is used and Anne’s great ability of mind-reading is shown through her acute observation of other characters’ facial expressions, gestures and behavior. Austen’s readers also receive additional knowledge about the main heroine through her body language (mainly looks and blushes). We may notice her gradual ‘expansion’ of speech; from her silence in the initial chapters, through her scarce remarks until she gradually becomes the focalizer in the novel, with her focalization ‘expanding’ in the second half. In the climactic scene in the penultimate chapter Anne finally presents herself as ‘somebody’. In her long and passionate conversation, she presents courageously her own opinions on women and their unprivileged situation.
