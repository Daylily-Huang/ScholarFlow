01 · 稀有物种难抓，取水提供了另一条取证路线。

同一份水里，为什么会有两种难题？

目标鱼留下的DNA少，近缘鱼留下的却可能很多。浓集一份水会同时收进两者，不能只靠加大水量提高目标所占比例。

所以先区分两个问题：灵敏度问目标很少时能否检出，特异性问信号是不是来自想找的物种。只测纯目标最低浓度，回答不了混合背景下能否认准。

02 · “有曲线”背后，既有DNA复制，也有荧光报告。

一个荧光信号要经过哪两道环节？

两条引物启动小片段复制，内部探针带报告基团和淬灭基团。完整时信号受压，聚合酶延伸切开探针后，报告基团与淬灭基团分离，荧光增加。

起始目标多时较早越过阈值，Ct较小，再和已知标准比较估算初始量。如果近亲也参与扩增或贡献微弱荧光，这条换算关系就可能被扰乱。

03 · 多一道探针并不会自动消除近亲DNA的影响。

区别放在探针端，还是引物端？

BRK1优先扩大探针区的种间差异：近亲可能被引物复制，但希望探针不报告它。BRK2和BUT1则优先扩大引物区差异，尽量从复制开头就减弱近亲进入扩增。

这就产生一个可检验的预期：当近亲很多时，前一种策略可能让近亲争用引物，而后一种策略更可能保住目标信号。原文没有把错配数当万能开关，后面还检查错在什么位置。

04 · 少模板时，同一浓度不同反应也可能有成有败。

低到每管两份，能找到多少次？

灵敏度实验只用BRK2，测试纯组织、近缘混样和两条真实溪流水样。作者先用合成标准估浓度，再做多管重复，以45轮内越线的反应比例估计成功率。

0.5拷贝/微升每次加4微升，平均就是2拷贝/反应；四来源单管成功率约71.9%–86.2%。三重复提高至少一次检出的机会，但并没有测出“只取一次水就能找到溪流所有稀鱼”的概率。

低浓度能检出之后，还须确认大量近亲不会把这份本领破坏。

05 · 单独不明显发光的近亲，仍可能在同管争用扩增资源。

为什么纯样好好的，混起来却变了？

把目标按1:10和1:100放进近亲DNA后，BRK1在牛红点鲑背景下曲线变弱、Ct还可能提前，从而既影响有无判读，也可能高估目标量。BRK2在相应背景表现更稳。

但BUT1遇湖红点鲑仍受干扰，说明不能只数错配总数。引物从3′端延伸，末端附近错配往往更有影响；因此湖红点鲑背景中的5处差异也未必胜过另一体系位置更有效的3处差异。

设计必须回到具体物种与反应里测试，而不是选一个“错配最多”的数字就结束。

06 · 实验室认出DNA之后，还剩生态来源这一层。

最后怎样把检测结果用于监测？

作者的实际建议是：比较本地目标与非目标序列，重视引物错配位置，再用纯样及不同背景比例的混样实测。现场取样量和技术重复仍重要，它们解决收集不足与随机漏检，而特异设计解决另一类干扰。

杂交后代也可能携带目标线粒体标记，捕食者还可能搬运猎物DNA。因此“体系认准了分子”与“现场有活的目标种”要由不同证据支撑。

整篇围绕一个朴素问题收束：既把稀少目标找出来，也查清信号是谁、从哪里来。

原文与译文
Environmental DNA (eDNA) is being rapidly adopted as a tool to detect rare animals. Quantitative PCR (qPCR) using probe-based chemistries may represent a particularly powerful tool because of the method’s sensitivity, specificity, and potential to quantify target DNA. However, there has been little work understanding the performance of these assays in the presence of closely related, sympatric taxa. If related species cause any cross-amplification or interference, false positives and negatives may be generated. These errors can be disastrous if false positives lead to overestimate the abundance of an endangered species or if false negatives prevent detection of an invasive species. In this study we test factors that influence the specificity and sensitivity of TaqMan MGB assays using co-occurring, closely related brook trout (Salvelinus fontinalis) and bull trout (S. confluentus) as a case study. We found qPCR to be substantially more sensitive than traditional PCR, with a high probability of detection at concentrations as low as 0.5 target copies/µl. We also found that number and placement of base pair mismatches between the Taqman MGB assay and non-target templates was important to target specificity, and that specificity was most influenced by base pair mismatches in the primers, rather than in the probe. We found that insufficient specificity can result in both false positive and false negative results, particularly in the presence of abundant related species. Our results highlight the utility of qPCR as a highly sensitive eDNA tool, and underscore the importance of careful assay design.
环境DNA（eDNA）正被迅速用于检出稀有动物。采用探针化学的定量PCR（qPCR）因灵敏、特异及潜在定量能力而尤具潜力，但当样本含有近缘同域类群时，其表现少有研究。近缘种若发生交叉扩增或干扰，就可能产生假阳性及假阴性：前者可使濒危种数量被高估，后者可导致漏掉入侵种。本研究以同域近缘的美洲红点鲑（Salvelinus fontinalis）和牛红点鲑（S. confluentus）为例，测试TaqMan MGB体系特异性及灵敏性的影响因素。我们发现qPCR比传统PCR灵敏得多，目标浓度低至0.5拷贝/µl仍有较高检出概率。检测体系与非目标模板间碱基错配的数量和位置均影响特异性，且引物错配的影响大于探针错配。特异性不足可同时造成假阳性和假阴性，尤其在近缘种丰富时。结果凸显qPCR作为高灵敏eDNA工具的价值，也强调仔细设计检测体系的重要性。

白话解释

一瓶溪水里，想找的鱼可能只留下少量DNA，旁边近亲鱼的DNA却很多。检测器既要“听得见小声”，还要“认得准是谁”。作者分别测试这两件事，发现高灵敏度不自动带来高特异性；近缘鱼还会干扰目标信号。
逻辑要点
  • 稀有目标与大量近缘背景共同构成真实检测难题。
  • 稀释实验测能否检出，纯样与混样实验测能否认准。
  • 错配的位置与数量改变干扰，因而灵敏度和特异性必须一起设计。

原文与译文
Environmental DNA (eDNA) is DNA extracted from an environmental sample without isolating the target organism [1], [2]. Environmental DNA has been characterized as a mixture of genomic DNA from many different organisms, which is often degraded into small fragments [2]. The collection and analysis of eDNA has many applications; one that has recently received a great deal of attention is the detection of rare aquatic vertebrates, both for the early detection of invading nonnative species (e.g., [3]–[6]) and for the detection of rare native species of interest (e.g., [7]–[9]) such as species currently listed under the U.S. Endangered Species Act.
eDNA是从环境样本中、不分离目标生物而取得的DNA[1,2]，常为许多生物基因组DNA的混合物，并已降解成小片段[2]。其应用众多，近期受到关注的是稀有水生脊椎动物检测，包括及早发现外来入侵种[3–6]，以及寻找稀少的本地保护物种[7–9]，如当时列入美国《濒危物种法》的种类。

白话解释

水样不像一块鱼鳍，能先确定是谁再提DNA。它把许多生物的碎片一起带进实验室，而研究者要从这锅混合物里认出某一种稀少的鱼。
逻辑要点
  • 环境取样省去抓取个体，却同时引入混合、碎片化模板。
  • 检测稀有物种因此要求从复杂背景中筛出微弱目标。

Much has been written about the problems associated with nonnative species, from economic to ecological impacts (e.g., [10], [11]). Nonnative species pose a particular threat to closely related native species with which they may compete or hybridize (e.g., [12], [13]). Consequently, they, and the closely related native species that they impact, are often the target of extensive monitoring programs. 逻辑要点

Along with its unique advantages, eDNA monitoring presents a unique set of challenges and limitations. For example, typical non-invasive DNA sampling targets a particular species by sampling hair, scat or other tissues deposited by the target organism. Thus, most of the DNA will either be from the target species, or from distantly related species (e.g., bacteria or prey species). Stream systems also pose special challenges for eDNA analyses. In lentic systems (e.g., ponds and lakes), equilibrium DNA concentrations are determined primarily by the rate of new DNA entering the water and the rate of DNA leaving the system through degradation [8], [14]. In lotic systems (e.g., streams and rivers), downstream transport likely causes DNA concentrations to be much lower than in lentic systems [8], especially if the target species is rare. 原文与译文
Quantitative PCR (qPCR) using TaqMan™ assays with minor groove binding probes (TaqMan MGB; Applied Biosystems – Life Technologies Corporation) may be a particularly useful tool for eDNA. TaqMan MGB assays use two primers to amplify a small section of target template. Nested within this amplicon is a binding site for a probe, which is labeled with a fluorescent reporter dye on one end and a quencher molecule on the other. When intact, the quencher absorbs the reporter dye signal via fluorescent resonance energy transfer (FRET). The probe is cleaved by the 5′–3′ nuclease activity of the DNA polymerase during extension, thus separating the quencher and resulting in an increased fluorescent signal. As a result, the level of fluorescence is proportional to the quantity of template. As PCR progresses and quantity of amplicon increases, so does the level of fluorescence, creating an amplification curve where fluorescence increases rapidly early in PCR, but slows to a plateau as reagents are consumed. The greater the initial quantity of target template, the earlier in PCR this curve becomes detectable. The cycle number at which the curve becomes clearly distinguishable from the background fluorescence is called the Ct, and it can be compared to standards of known copy number to accurately estimate initial target quantity in the unknown sample [15]. This method is known to be highly sensitive. For example, previous research used repeated dilutions to assess the sensitivity of traditional PCR tests for eDNA detection of Asian carp (Hypophthalmichthys molitrix and H. nobilis) [5], [16]. The researchers determined that these species could be detected at concentrations of 7 copies/µL of DNA for H. molitrix and 207 copies/µL for H. nobilis using 1 µL template in traditional PCR reactions [5], [16]. In comparison, qPCR has been shown to have reasonable probabilities of detection at concentrations as low as 0.5 copy/µL [17].
TaqMan MGB qPCR可能尤其适用。两条引物扩增目标小片段，其中有一处探针结合位点；探针一端带荧光报告基团，另一端带淬灭基团，完整时经FRET抑制荧光。聚合酶延伸时以5′–3′核酸酶活性切开探针，分开淬灭基团，荧光增加。因此荧光随模板量增加；随PCR进行，扩增曲线先快速上升，再因试剂消耗进入平台。起始模板越多，越早可见曲线；明显超过背景的循环数称Ct，可与已知拷贝数标准比较，估计未知样本的初始量[15]。该方法很灵敏：传统PCR对亚洲鲢鳙eDNA的稀释实验检出浓度分别为7及207拷贝/µl（每反应1 µl模板）[5,16]，而qPCR在0.5拷贝/µl已有合理检出概率[17]。

白话解释

可以把探针想成带着小灯与遮光罩的一段短链。完整时灯光被压住，延伸切开后才亮；每轮多出产物，信号跟着积累。起始目标多，较早越过检测线，Ct就小。关键是整套过程既需要引物启动复制，也需要探针正确报告。
逻辑要点
  • 引物负责启动扩增，探针被切开后才产生增强的荧光。
  • 产物积累形成曲线，起始量越大通常越早越过阈值。
  • 通过已知标准比较Ct，才可把越线时间换成初始拷贝数估计。

TaqMan MGB probes should also be substantially more target-specific than traditional PCR. Minor groove binding probes use a 3′ modification to allow construction of very short probes and are extremely sensitive to base pair mismatches [18]. These types of assays have been used for several recent eDNA studies [8], [19]–[21]. However, even TaqMan MGB probes can produce measureable signals with two or three base pair mismatches [22]–[24]. Location of the base pair mismatches within the probe [18] and the primer sets [25] are important to assay specificity. Thus, when using TaqMan MGB assays, accurate results rely on careful assay design, especially when the target species represents a small proportion of the total sample DNA and a closely related species represents the majority.

原文与译文
When the target species (e.g., bull trout) is rare relative to sympatric, closely related species (e.g., brook trout), designing a target-specific assay is particularly important. We found that assay specificity was most influenced by base pair mismatches in the primers, rather than mismatches in the probe. We also found that the location of these mismatches was important. In general, as primer base pair mismatches increased, fluorescence of non-target templates decreased (Figure 1) and non-target template competition became weaker. When brook trout DNA made up ∼0.01 of the total in mixtures between brook and bull trout, the fluorescent signal was substantially reduced in assay BRK1 (Figure 2a), which has three base-pair differences in the probe but only one in the primers (Table 1). This probably results from the bull trout DNA template competing for the primers, producing an amplification curve which is not consistently distinguishable from bull trout alone. In contrast, BRK2 and BUT1 have nine and eight base pair differences in the primers, respectively, but only one in the probe, and amplified as well in mixtures with bull and brook trout as they did in isolation (Figure 2c and 3b).
当目标稀少而近缘同域种丰富时，特异设计尤其重要。引物错配比探针错配更影响特异性，错配位置也重要。总体上，引物错配增加时非目标荧光下降（图1），竞争减弱。美洲DNA仅占混样约0.01时，探针3处、引物仅1处错配的BRK1信号显著降低（图2a、表1）；作者认为可能是牛红点鲑争用引物，曲线难以和纯牛红点鲑区分。BRK2及BUT1的引物分别有9与8处差异、探针各仅1处，却在对方背景中与纯样扩增表现相当（图2c、3b）。

Base pair mismatch location in the primers also influenced our results. The BUT1 assay was strongly affected in mixed bull/lake trout samples, even though there are more base pair mismatches between the BUT1 assay and lake trout (5) than between BRK1 and lake trout (3), which was unaffected by brook/lake trout mixtures. Base pair mismatches near the 3′ end of the primer have a much larger impact on specificity than those in other regions, and 5′ mismatches generally have very little effect on specificity (e.g., [25]). The BRK1 assay has a base pair mismatch with lake trout in the last 2 base pairs of the 3′ end, while the BUT1 assay base pair mismatches are further from the 3′ end (Table 1). If eDNA is to be used as a standard monitoring tool for streams, high sample portability and the ability to preserve samples in the field will be necessary. In lentic systems, detection of eDNA of target organisms from small amounts of water (e.g., 15 mL) has been successful [4], [8]. In streams, the DNA density of target organisms is likely to be lower because many streams are partially fed by groundwater, exposure to target organisms shedding DNA is brief, and photo-degradation of DNA is more likely because streams are shallow and well-mixed. Consequently, relatively large volumes of water may need to be sampled to concentrate an adequate DNA sample. In-situ filtering permits adequate sampling without the need to transport water samples. Furthermore, DNA trapped on a filter is easier to protect from degradation than DNA suspended in large volumes of water. We used ice to keep samples cold in the field, but other approaches exist (e.g., storage in 95% EtOH [7]). While brook trout were common in both of the streams we sampled, the number of copies obtained from these samples was several orders of magnitude greater than necessary to obtain reliable species identification. The final 100 µL DNA solution from our streams contained approximately 50,000 and 10,000 target copies for Plant Creek and Miller Creek, respectively.

实际监测不一定要背6升水回实验室，可以在溪边让水穿过滤膜，再带走滤膜。这里两条溪的目标鱼原本常见，所以真实水样证明能取得大量信号；真正很稀的场景仍需结合取水量和检测概率来设计。
逻辑要点
  • 流水环境可能需要更大体积，现场过滤解决浓集和运输。
  • 本次水样信号充足，为低浓度系列提供材料，但不是稀有种现场检出率的直接验证。

原文与译文
Environmental DNA detection may be especially useful in situations where the target species is too rare to be identified using alternative approaches, or where alternative sampling approaches are unreasonably expensive or labor-intensive. However, these are also the circumstances where it is most important to understand the likelihood of false positives and false negatives. False negatives can occur because the quantity of target species eDNA collected falls below a detection threshold or because non-target substances interfere with the assay. Pumping large water samples and capitalizing on the extreme sensitivity of qPCR minimizes the risk of false negatives, as would setting the sampling scheme in a formal occupancy estimation framework (e.g., [36]). However, in this study we identified non-target template competition as another possible confounding factor. Insufficiently specific primers and probes can result in both false positives – particularly if we interpret any amplification as a positive result – and false negatives in the case of strong non-target template competition. We also found that non-target template competition strongly skewed Ct values, which produces inaccurate DNA quantity estimates and could lead to an inaccurate estimate of fish abundance (e.g., [19]). Furthermore, in the case of strong non-target template competition, sequencing PCR products will probably be an ineffective screen for assay specificity because the majority of products will be derived from the non-target template. Based on findings from this study, the best solution to these issues is (1) to design assays that maximize primer region base pair mismatches with non-target species, and (2) to experimentally test assays against pure and mixed samples of target and non-target DNA. Pre-screening eDNA assays against mixtures of target and non-target DNA is simple and relatively inexpensive, has been done in some other eDNA studies [7], and requires little additional effort; testing against non-target DNA is already standard in eDNA studies (e.g, [8]). There is a high cost associated with failing to detect an endangered or invasive species, so even when closely related taxa are not anticipated in the system, we believe that it is in best interests of future studies to carefully test assays against pure and mixed samples from common local species.
eDNA最适用于目标太少、其他方法难以或代价过高的情况，也最需了解假阳性及假阴性。取水不足、低于检测线及非目标物质干扰都可致漏检；大体积过滤、qPCR灵敏性及占域估计设计[36]可减轻部分风险。本文另识别出非目标模板竞争：特异性不足的引物与探针既会使任何扩增都被误判为阳性，也会在强竞争下造成漏检；Ct偏移还会误估DNA量及鱼数量[19]。强竞争时，扩增产物测序可能主要读出非目标，难以有效筛查特异性。因此建议增大与非目标的引物区错配，并用目标、非目标的纯样及混样实测。混样预筛简单、便宜且已有先例[7]；即使预期无近缘种，也应测试常见本地物种，因为漏检保护种或入侵种的代价很高。

白话解释

增加水量解决的是“有没有收进足够模板”，增加重复解决的是“这一管会不会碰巧失败”；若检测体系本身认错或被近亲干扰，这两招都补不全。作者最终建议把纯样与混样测试列为上场前的实际试车。
逻辑要点
  • 低量、取样遗漏和模板竞争是不同误差来源，需要不同补救。
  • 竞争还能扭曲Ct，故单看是否亮或事后测序不充分。
  • 序列设计与纯样/混样实测合起来，才构成本文建议的检测验证。

原文与译文
Hybridization poses an additional consideration when using these assays. Brook trout and bull trout are able to hybridize [37]. Because we are using mitochondrial markers, hybrid individuals could cause us to incorrectly conclude that our target species is present (e.g., detect brook trout when only bull trout and brook/bull trout hybrids are present), leading to an over-estimate of target species distribution. This problem should be less important when there is not extensive back-crossing (e.g., [38]), which appears to be true for brook trout and bull trout [37], [39]. As a result, we think that presence of mitochondrial DNA of brook trout or bull trout is indicative of species presence. This issue may be more substantial for species that more readily introgress, such as cutthroat trout and rainbow trout [12].
杂交还需考虑。两种红点鲑能杂交[37]，而本文检测线粒体标记，杂交个体可能让人误以为纯目标种存在，例如只有牛红点鲑与杂交后代时仍检出美洲标记，从而高估其分布。若没有广泛回交[38]，问题较小；作者认为该对物种似属此情形[37,39]，因此将其线粒体DNA视为物种出现的指标。对更易渐渗的割喉鳟与虹鳟[12]，问题可能更严重。

白话解释

检测器可以完全认对一段线粒体DNA，却仍没回答“水里是不是有纯种目标鱼”。杂交后代也可能带着母系的这段标记，所以分子身份和整条鱼的分类身份还隔着一步。
逻辑要点
  • 线粒体标记可经杂交进入后代，检出序列不必对应纯目标个体。
  • 回交和渐渗程度决定这种解释问题有多大。

原文与译文
The hybridization issue illustrates the more general problem that target DNA presence may not always indicate target species presence. Besides hybridization, false positives may result from the extreme sensitivity of eDNA monitoring. Even when diluting to an average of two target copies/reaction, we had better than 70% amplification success with stream-derived DNA samples. This represents such a small quantity of template that detected DNA could derive from some source other than a live organism. Contamination by researchers in the field or lab is an obvious issue, but there are other possibilities as well. For example, the feces of piscivorous birds or mammals may contain prey DNA [40], and these animals may transport this DNA far from its point of origin. This issue has been a concern for on-going eDNA projects [41], and there have been previous suggestions to place a higher threshold on the acceptable copy number representing a positive identification [42]. Environmental DNA collection to detect rare organisms is relatively new, and we currently have no idea how much background DNA exists in the environment. Further studies to determine expected quantities of DNA in freshwater systems given a specific target organism biomass will help set these detection thresholds.
杂交说明更一般的问题：目标DNA出现未必等于目标物种出现。极灵敏的eDNA还可能检出非活体来源：每反应平均2拷贝时，溪水DNA仍>70%扩增成功。除了野外或实验室污染，食鱼鸟兽粪便也可含猎物DNA[40]并搬运至远处，相关项目已有担忧[41]，有人建议提高阳性鉴定所需拷贝阈值[42]。当时尚不清楚环境背景DNA有多少；进一步研究特定生物量对应的淡水DNA量，有助于设定检测阈值。

白话解释

最后一层判断发生在实验室之外：认准了DNA，还要问它怎么来到这份水。活鱼、杂交后代、捕食者带来的残留，都可能留下同一个靶标；要把分子信号变成分布结论，还得结合现场和来源。
逻辑要点
  • 高灵敏度让极少量残留也可被发现。
  • 因此体系特异性解决分子辨认，生态来源证据再支持物种现场出现的解释。
