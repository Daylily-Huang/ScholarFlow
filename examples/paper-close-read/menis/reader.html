为什么不满足于“他一时失控”

宽恕的理由可以是“这人做错了，但控制能力一时受损”；正当化的理由却可能暗示“对方先做了足够严重的错事”。两种理由都可能减责，却把过错放在不同位置。

04 · 从个人暴躁转向社会如何评男人

“支配性男性气质”究竟在解释什么

想象两个相互冲突的要求：一个丈夫得维护家庭权威；一个体面的男人又应保护被视为脆弱的女人。本文所谓支配性男性气质，关注这类在特定社会获得优势的男性规范，而不是给所有男人贴一个性格标签。

因此，保护女性与维持等级可能同时出现。反对严罚通奸的议员，也可能以女人软弱、缺乏理性作理由；意见虽不同，仍共享谁有判断权、谁需要被管理的预设。这个框架允许内部冲突，才有可能解释法案为什么没有一路照同一方向发展。

原文与译文
The Mawgridge’s case in 1707 set the precedent where adultery was recognised as a justified trigger for the husband’s killing of his wife’s lover; this crystallised a partial defence for provocation. However, in an 1810 case, the killing of the unfaithful wife followed a manslaughter conviction rather than murder for the first time. This study aims to investigate the shaping of a legally permissible violence, that is, the mitigation of the husband’s culpability in killing his adulterous wife. This provides the opportunity to question the (ir)rationality behind the judiciary’s discourse in the case of R v Clinton 2012; here, despite infidelity being abolished in 2009 in England and Wales as a defence for murder, the judges still insisted on its relevance in our culture and hence on legal culpability. The theoretical framework in this paper draws upon the scholarship of masculinity, the family, and the law. This paper discusses the contribution of the hegemonic male identity in creating this legal violence and fortifying social-hierarchical structure.
1707年的 Mawgridge 案确立了先例：丈夫杀死妻子的情人，通奸可被认作挑衅诱因，形成部分抗辩。但到1810年的一个案件，丈夫杀死不忠妻子，首次被判 manslaughter 而非 murder。本文研究这种“法律容许的暴力”如何形成，即杀妻丈夫的罪责为何被减轻，并借此质疑2012年 R v Clinton 案的司法论述：英格兰与威尔士2009年已排除不忠作为杀人抗辩的理由，法官仍坚持其文化和归责意义。文章借男性气质、家庭与法律研究，讨论支配性男性身份如何参与这种法律暴力的建构，并强化社会等级。

白话解释

先抓住那一步变化：早先减责的对象是杀情人的丈夫，1810年却出现杀妻也减责。作者不只问法条怎样改，更问“丈夫受了这样的委屈可以少担点责”为什么会让人觉得说得通。
逻辑要点
  • 由杀情人到杀妻，界定本文需要解释的变化。
  • 把减责放回社会性别秩序，提出贯穿全文的解释。
  • 2012年的案件提供追问历史来源的入口。

原文与译文
The criminal case of R v Clinton (2012) EWCA Crim 2 drew attention to a feature debated in Parliament when reforming the law of the partial defence of provocation in 2009. At the report stage of the Coroners and Justice Bill 2009, the Lords considered whether the new legislation needed to explicitly exclude ‘sexual infidelity’ as a qualifying trigger to the defence. Those who supported the explicit exclusion, such as Lord Bach, argued that ‘killing in response to sexual infidelity is, well into the twenty-first century, not a defensible basis for’ a reduction in culpability.1 This exclusion was made, and the new law reads as follows: ‘In determining whether a loss of self-control had a qualifying trigger […] the fact that a thing done or said constituted sexual infidelity is to be disregarded’.2 The Court of Appeal’s judgment in Clinton then came as a surprise.
2012年 R v Clinton 案使人重新关注2009年挑衅部分抗辩改革。审议《验尸官与司法法案》时，上院讨论是否明确排除“性不忠”作为抗辩诱因。支持排除的 Bach 勋爵说，到21世纪，因不忠杀人已不能成为减责的合理基础（注1）。立法最终规定：判断失控是否有合格诱因时，应忽略构成性不忠的言行（注2）。因此，上诉法院在 Clinton 案中的判断令人意外。

白话解释

立法者想堵住的口子很明确：不能因为伴侣不忠，就把杀人说得轻一些。可接下来的案件让作者怀疑，这个口子是不是换了名字又被打开了。

原文与译文
Jon-Jacques Clinton killed his wife by beating her with a wooden baton and strangling her with a belt; the cause of death was head injury and asphyxia caused by a ligature compression of the neck.3 Trial Judge Smith refused to allow the defence. She concluded that, despite the admission of infidelity and the accompanied taunts preceding the killing, this was not enough to constitute the defence of loss of control, certainly not after infidelity being specifically excluded by the recent legislation. However, the Court of Appeal concluded differently. Lord Judge, Mr. Justice Henriques, and Mrs. Justice Gloster considered whether, although sexual infidelity is explicitly excluded as a trigger for a loss of control defence, it could still be taken as evidence suggesting the gravity of the context in which the killing took place. They argued that ‘the idea that, in the search for a qualifying trigger, the context in which such words are used should be ignored represents an artificiality which the administration of criminal justice should do without’.4 Indeed, a new trial was ordered, where the defence of loss of control was left to the jury.
Clinton 用木棍殴打并用腰带勒死妻子，死因为头部损伤和颈部受压窒息（注3）。初审法官 Smith 不允许失控抗辩：即使死前承认不忠并嘲讽，也不足以构成抗辩，尤其立法已明确排除不忠。但上诉法院的三位法官认为，不忠虽不能是诱因，仍可帮助说明杀人时情境的严重性；寻找合格诱因时完全忽略这些话的语境，会制造刑事司法不应有的人为性（注4）。法院命令重审，让陪审团考虑失控抗辩。

关键是“诱因”和“背景”的区别：前者被排除了，后者却仍可能左右判断。本文由这一步转向历史，追问为何不忠在归责时总有那么大的分量。

原文与译文
The Clinton case attracted some attention because, as better put by Horder and Fitz-Gibbon, the judiciary treated the change in the law as a technicality instead of regarding ‘the change as entailing or demanding a more general shift in moral thinking concerning the relative seriousness of murders committed in response to sexual infidelity’ (Horder and Fitz-Gibbon 2015, p. 309). Indeed, the case opens the opportunity to reflect on the possibility that past cultural attitudes on adultery have left a social memory echoing well into the twenty-first century. Moreover, apart from the contrasting views expressed in Parliament in the debate concerning the legislative exclusion of sexual infidelity, some legal scholars also felt the need to defend the existence of infidelity as a trigger to loss of control. In his memorandum to the House of Commons at the Committee Stage to the Coroners and Justice Bill, Horder considered that the exclusion of infidelity as a trigger should not be absolute because there may be ‘rare meritorious cases’ which should not be liable for murder (cited in Slater 2012, p. 165).
Horder 与 Fitz-Gibbon 指出，司法把改革当作技术调整，没有把它理解成对不忠杀人严重性的道德观念转变（2015，第309页）。案件因而让人追问，过去对通奸的文化态度是否作为社会记忆延续到21世纪。议会和法律学者也并非一致支持彻底排除：Horder 提交的意见认为，可能存在少数有理据、不应按 murder 归责的案件，所以不应绝对排除不忠（转引 Slater 2012，第165页）。

作者看到的不是“所有人意见都一样”，而是冲突：有人想改变看待这种杀人的方式，有人觉得仍得给少数案件留余地。要解释这种分歧，她准备往历史里找。

原文与译文
This study aims to investigate the construction of a legally permissible violence, that is, the mitigation of the husband’s culpability in killing his adulterous wife. Kesselring identified a 1600 case as the first instance where a judicial discussion took place concerning the question of whether a provoked retaliation could be taken as mitigation for murder and thus reduce the defendant’s culpability (Kesselring 2016, p. 207). It is only in a 1671 case, involving the killing of the wife’s lover by the husband, that adultery was first considered as a possible provocative trigger (ibid., p. 208). The precedent was established following the Mawgridge’s case in 1707 (ibid., p. 207), and then it was engraved into the first modern criminal law textbook, Blackstone’s Commentaries on the Laws of England (Blackstone 1766). Finally, it is with an 1810 case where, for the first time (as far as accessible data could reveal), the killing of the unfaithful wife followed a conviction of manslaughter, rather than murder. This paper will only concentrate on the early 1800s; it will take the 1810 Richard Griffin case as a departure point for the analysis.
本文研究杀死不忠妻子的丈夫如何获得减责。Kesselring 将1600年案件视为司法最早讨论受挑衅报复能否减轻杀人罪责的例子；1671年才首次把妻子通奸视作丈夫杀死情人的可能诱因；1707年 Mawgridge 案确立先例，随后进入 Blackstone 的《英国法释义》（文中引1766）。可及资料所见，到1810年才首次出现杀死不忠妻子而判 manslaughter 的案件。本文聚焦十九世纪初，以 Richard Griffin 的1810年案件为出发点。

白话解释

这不是一项自古就有的“丈夫权利”。把几个时间点分开，你会看到：先有一般的挑衅减责，再有杀情人的通奸抗辩，更晚才看到杀妻减责。需要解释的正是后一步。
逻辑要点
  • 区分一般挑衅抗辩、杀情人减责与杀妻减责，避免把不同历史合成一条古老传统。
  • 1810年作为可见材料中的起点，限定全文研究范围。

This study is interdisciplinary; it examines the law doctrine and legislation and the period’s socio-political and cultural mentality. The law that made adultery punishable by death in England for a short period of time was the Act for Suppressing the Detestable Sins of Incest, Adultery, and Fornication 1650 (British History 1911b). The Act made adultery a criminal offence, to be dealt with by the Magistrates rather than by the Church courts. The humanitarian and equality messages this movement attempted to put across (Scott 2000, p. 260) were overshadowed by accompanying messages of sexual transgression.
