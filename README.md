# ScholarFlow
An evidence-grounded AI research workflow for literature discovery, evidence extraction, and controversy-aware synthesis.

## Problem statement variants
- LitTriad — Three composable AI skills for literature discovery, evidence extraction, and scientific synthesis.
- EvidenceScholar — Open-source AI skills for reproducible literature search, evidence extraction, and academic controversy synthesis.
- ResearchEvidencePipeline — A modular AI pipeline for literature retrieval, evidence-grounded extraction, and multi-paper synthesis.
- SciSynthesis — AI skills for finding scientific literature, extracting verifiable evidence, and mapping consensus, controversies, and research gaps.
